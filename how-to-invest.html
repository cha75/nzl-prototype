How to invest inNZ Rural Land Co (NZL)

You can own shares in two ways.

01

Invest online yourself

With Sharesies, you buy and manage the shares yourself.

  1. Open an account
  2. Search for NZL
  3. Choose how much to invest and buy
Go to Sharesies
02

Invest through a broker

A broker can advise you and place the order for you.

  1. Contact Craigs Investment Partners or Jarden
  2. Ask to buy New Zealand Rural Land Company shares (NZX: NZL)
  3. Your broker buys them on your behalf
Craigs Jarden

NZL shares can be bought through any NZX broker or investing platform.

NZX code
NZL
