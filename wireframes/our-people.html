How NZ Rural Land Co (NZL) is run

NZ Rural Land Co (NZL) is listed on the NZX. Our board of directors oversees the company. An external manager, New Zealand Rural Land Management (NZRLM), runs it day to day under a Management Agreement. NZRLM is 100% owned by Allied Farmers Limited. Our accountants are Deloitte, our auditors are PwC and our share registry is MUFG.

Our board

Rob Campbell

Independent Chair
Appointed September 2020

Rob has more than 40 years' experience in investment management and corporate governance. He is Chair of Ara Ake and Chancellor of Auckland University of Technology. He has previously chaired SkyCity Entertainment Group, Summerset Group, Tourism Holdings and WEL Networks. Rob trained as an economist and began his career teaching economic history at Victoria University of Wellington. He is a Chartered Fellow of the Institute of Directors and was made a Companion of the New Zealand Order of Merit (CNZM) in 2019 for services to governance and business.

Sarah Kennedy

Independent Director
Appointed April 2020

Sarah is the founder and CEO of Calocurb, a direct-to-consumer company focused on weight management. She has held executive roles at Lifestream International and Designer Textiles. From 2011 to 2014 she held senior roles at Fonterra, including Vice President of International Farming and Managing Director of RD1, Fonterra's rural retail chain. Before Fonterra, Sarah spent 10 years as Managing Director of Healtheries/Vitaco. She is a trained veterinarian.

Tia Greenaway

Independent Director
Appointed X

Tia (Ngāti Tūwharetoa, Waikato-Tainui) leads the Rautaki Māori team at He Pou a Rangi, the Climate Change Commission.
